The Method

The Reality Rehearsal Method

A structured process for making the fulfilled state vivid, emotionally natural, and easy to revisit.

Neville's metaphysical teaching is presented as a spiritual and philosophical practice. We do not guarantee external outcomes or claim scientific proof of manifestation.

Live From the State
Notice the choices and behaviors that become more available.
Update the Vision
Mark what is realized, evolved, in progress, or no longer desired.
