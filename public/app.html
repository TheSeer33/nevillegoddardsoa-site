StateShift is a simple Neville-inspired practice app for people who do not need more vague affirmation content. Use it before sleep, record the state change, and keep the routine clean.

How it works

Pick a focus, describe one short scene that would happen after your desire is fulfilled, and set the timer. When the session ends, record how settled and convinced you feel. Your scene and practice history stay on this device unless you choose to clear them.
