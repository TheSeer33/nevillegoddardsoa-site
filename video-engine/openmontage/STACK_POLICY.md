# Neville Goddard SOA — OpenMontage Production Policy

OpenMontage is the exclusive video-production engine for Neville Goddard SOA long-form and short-form video production.

Upstream: https://github.com/calesthio/OpenMontage
License: GNU AGPLv3

## Non-negotiable constraints
- Do not use HeyGen.
- Do not use Runway or other paid video-generation providers.
- Do not use paid avatar generators.
- Prefer OpenMontage's free/open-source/local paths.
- Prefer real motion footage from free/open sources over slideshow-style video.
- Prefer Piper/local TTS when viable.
- Use FFmpeg / Remotion composition and OpenMontage-native quality gates.
- Generate thumbnails, captions, audio mix, SEO packaging, shorts, and long-form assets through the OpenMontage workflow where supported.
- Do not publish a render that fails visual, audio, subtitle, pacing, or delivery-promise review.

## Quality target
Aim for best-in-class YouTube quality: strong first 5 seconds, pattern interrupts, narrative escalation, clear teaching, cinematic visual continuity, readable captions, broadcast-safe audio, high-retention pacing, and differentiated creative direction.

"Top 0.01%" and "millions of views" are creative targets, not guarantees. Performance is measured from actual CTR, retention, watch time, saves/shares, subscriber conversion and revenue.

## Default free stack
- OpenMontage orchestration
- Piper TTS (local/free) or another free-tier voice source only when truly free
- Free/open stock and archival motion footage
- FFmpeg
- Remotion
- Whisper/WhisperX if locally available
- Open-source/local image generation for thumbnails when available
- Free music/SFX sources with commercial-use rights

## Editorial rules
- Do not impersonate Neville Goddard.
- If narration is modern/original, label it as Neville-inspired rather than an original Neville recording.
- Do not fabricate quotations.
- Distinguish metaphysical teachings from empirical claims.
- Never promise guaranteed financial outcomes.
