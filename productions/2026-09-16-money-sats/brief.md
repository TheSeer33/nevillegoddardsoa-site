# Production Brief

## Working title
How to Manifest Money With SATS: Neville Goddard's "Already Done" Method

## Core audience
People actively searching manifestation, money manifestation, Law of Assumption, SATS, financial abundance and Neville Goddard.

## Promise
By the end, the viewer understands why trying to "get money" can reinforce lack, how Neville's state-based approach reframes the practice, and how to build one short SATS scene that implies financial relief is already true.

## Creative angle
Most manifestation videos tell viewers to repeat more affirmations. This video opens by challenging the chase itself: "What if the reason money still feels far away is that every technique you're doing keeps rehearsing the fact that you don't have it?"

## Retention architecture
0:00–0:20 Contrarian hook + unresolved loop
0:20–1:15 The hidden mistake: manifesting FROM lack
1:15–2:40 Neville's core idea: occupy the end state
2:40–4:15 Build a money SATS scene
4:15–5:35 The sensory detail most people overdo
5:35–6:45 What to do when the 3D contradicts you
6:45–8:15 Mental diet / identity during the day
8:15–9:20 Guided 60-second mini-SATS
9:20–10:15 Recap + CTA

## Visual language
1950s intellectual mysticism meets modern luxury editorial.
Warm lamplight, night city windows, journals, quiet bedroom, hands closing a laptop after seeing a paid invoice, phone balance notification recreated generically (no fake bank branding), sunrise, calm walking footage, understated abundance cues.
No cliché raining cash.

## Pacing
Cinematic but not sleepy. Dense visual changes, occasional text-only impact frames, kinetic diagrams for state -> assumption -> behavior/attention -> experience.

## Primary CTA
SATS Protocol Guide / Neville Goddard SOA website.

## Important
Do not claim the practice guarantees money. Present Neville's metaphysical framework accurately while avoiding fabricated certainty.
