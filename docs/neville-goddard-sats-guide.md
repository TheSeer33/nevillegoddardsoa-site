# Neville Goddard SATS Guide

SATS, commonly described as the State Akin to Sleep, is one of the best-known practices associated with Neville Goddard's Law of Assumption teaching. The practice centers on relaxing before sleep, selecting a short imaginal scene that would imply a desired outcome is already true, and returning to that scene until the assumed state feels natural.

## A simple SATS practice

1. Relax the body before sleep.
2. Choose one short, ordinary scene that implies the desired result is already accomplished.
3. Experience the scene from the first-person point of view.
4. Keep the scene simple rather than constantly changing it.
5. Return to the feeling of the fulfilled state instead of repeatedly checking external circumstances.

The purpose is not to force an emotion or guarantee an outcome. It is an educational practice in imagination, assumption, and self-concept.
